Runway

A free toolkit for layoffs in India. The numbers you enter stay on your device.

Losing a job is hard. You don’t have to work out the next step alone.

Runway breaks the first weeks into small, clear steps: what to ask, what to protect, who to write to, and how long your money will last. Start where you are.

A layoff is a business decision, not a verdict on you.

  • Made for India: ₹, provident fund, gratuity, notice periods.
  • Free, with no sign-up. The amounts you type stay on your device.
  • Built to be used in small steps, a few minutes at a time.
